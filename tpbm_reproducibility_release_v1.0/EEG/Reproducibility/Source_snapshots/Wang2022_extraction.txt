Notes:        Wang et al. used 64-channel EEG in 44 healthy adults to test whether right-forehead 1064-nm tPBM modulates specific EEG-derived brain networks rather than just global scalp power. They combined group singular value decomposition with eLORETA to identify 11 EEG brain-network components, then found that tPBM increased alpha power in networks overlapping with the DMN, executive control network, frontoparietal network, and visual network, while also decreasing gamma power in selected networks. The authors argue this provides a mechanistic EEG-network link between tPBM and previously reported cognitive benefits, though no direct cognitive task was measured in this study.
