D. tPBM Parameters
Device: CG-5000 Class IV laser, Cell Gen Therapeutics, Dallas, TX.
Wavelength: 1064 nm.
Mode: Continuous wave.
Target: Right prefrontal cortex, approximately 1 cm below Fp2.
Aperture: Circular, 4.2 cm diameter.
Nominal irradiance: 250 mW/cm2.
Effective measured irradiance: approximately 223 mW/cm2 after optical/diffuser losses.
Reported fluence: approximately 107 J/cm2 over 8 min based on the effective measured irradiance.
Nominal calculated fluence: 120 J/cm2 if 250 mW/cm2 is applied for the full 480 s; retain the paper's approximately 107 J/cm2 as the reported effective value and do not silently substitute the nominal calculation.
Duration: 8 min.
Sessions: Two experimental conditions per participant, active and sham, conducted on separate days.
Sham: Same device/setup, aperture occluded with a solid 5-mm opaque black PLA cap. Device remained powered at 0.1 W to match device hum. Post hoc power-meter testing found no detectable transmitted irradiance beneath the cap (approximately 3 microW/cm2 noise floor), indicating effectively zero delivered light.
Masking: Single-blind; goggles and device positioning were used to reduce visual and tactile cues.
Order: Counterbalanced across participants.
Co-interventions: None reported.


E. EEG / MEG Neurophysiology
EEG role: Primary mechanistic biomarker alongside MEG.
Acquisition state: Resting-state eyes open with fixation during pre and post recordings; EEG continued during stimulation.
Timeline: 6-min pre-stimulation simultaneous MEG/EEG, 8-min active or sham stimulation, 6-min post-stimulation simultaneous MEG/EEG.
EEG hardware: 64-channel Easycap, international 10-10 configuration; impedance maintained below 5 kOhm.
MEG hardware: MEGIN Neuromag TRIUX Neo; 102 magnetometers + 204 planar gradiometers.
Sampling: 1000 Hz at acquisition; downsampled to 500 Hz after preprocessing.
Preprocessing: Brainstorm; DC offset removal; 60- and 120-Hz notch filters; 0.5-150 Hz zero-phase Butterworth bandpass; signal-space projection for cardiac and ocular artifacts.
Structural imaging: Individual T1-weighted MRI used for co-registration/source modeling; three-layer boundary element model.
Source methods: sLORETA distributed source imaging; group SVD/ICA source isolation; equivalent-current-dipole modeling using simulated annealing plus local optimization.
Primary frequency bands: Alpha 8-12 Hz; beta 12-30 Hz.
Connectivity: Phase Transfer Entropy (PTE) for directional connectivity.
Cross-frequency analysis: Infra-slow phase-amplitude coupling (PAC) with alpha/beta amplitudes.
Statistical model: Paired-samples t-tests as the primary PTE statistic; 5000-iteration within-subject sign-flip permutation procedure; Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction q<0.05. Cohen's d was computed for significant connections.
PAC statistics: Surrogate-shuffle procedure using circular shifts of amplitude envelopes; significant bins retained at p<0.05.
Key qualitative findings: tPBM produced frequency-specific cortical reorganization, with alpha involving distributed frontal/visual/sensorimotor systems and beta involving executive, sensorimotor, and temporal association systems. Infra-slow rhythms significantly modulated alpha and beta amplitudes.
Reporting inconsistency flag: The narrative Results and Figure 2 caption do not list identical Brodmann areas for the pre-to-post DMN/CEN source-localization pattern. Treat the DMN-to-CEN interpretation as mechanistic/qualitative unless the source maps and supplementary analyses are independently reconciled.
Closed-loop use: None. EEG/MEG measured response but did not guide or adapt stimulation.

H. Quality / Risk-of-Bias Considerations
Comparator: Stronger than Pruitt 2024 because an active-vs-sham within-subject comparison is included.
Blinding: Single-blind.
Sequence allocation: Counterbalanced; exact randomization procedure unclear.
Within-subject design: Reduces between-person confounding but requires paired-effect variance handling.
Multiplicity: Extensive source-space, connectivity, and PAC analyses; FDR/permutation procedures were used, but multiple reported pathways are dependent and must not be treated as independent studies.
Selective pathway concern: Main text emphasizes selected significant PTE pathways; a prespecified effect-selection rule is needed for meta-analysis.
Effect-size convention RESOLVED BY AUTHOR on 26/08/2026: Subrat Bastola confirmed that the paired active-versus-sham PTE effects are Cohen's d_av, calculated as (M_active - M_sham) / sqrt[(SD_active^2 + SD_sham^2)/2], using participant-level condition means and marginal SDs.
Other limitation: No direct cognition, acute-only mechanistic endpoint, companion cohort with Pruitt 2024, and complex multistep source analysis.
Overall role: Controlled mechanistic EEG/MEG evidence with author-resolved d_av point estimates. Quantitative use is now permitted using the review's explicitly conservative variance route when paired correlation is unavailable; exclude/reweight in the paired-variance sensitivity.
