E. EEG / Neurophysiology
EEG used?: Yes.
EEG linked to cognition?: No; article explicitly states lack of direct cognitive association as a limitation.
Open/closed loop: Open-loop; EEG used as biomarker/analysis after fixed stimulation.
EEG type: Eyes-closed resting EEG with Emotiv Flex2; bilateral earlobe reference; 128-Hz sampling.
Timing: 5 min pre, 8 min tPBM, 5 min post; ~260±77 s delay for EEG quality checking after stimulation is reported.
Metrics: Four microstate classes; mean duration, occurrences/sec, time coverage, transition probabilities; microstate-specific wPLI connectivity; local/global efficiency/betweenness; machine-learning features.
EEG changed?: Yes. PW specifically increased microstate C mean duration and altered microstate-C network metrics with medium-to-large effect sizes versus controls.
Algorithm/model: MNE preprocessing; ICA; Pycrostates; wPLI networks; graph metrics; RBF support-vector machine + kernel SHAP. Classifier AUC=0.956 and 8/10 top features were microstate-C related.

F. Cognitive/Clinical Outcomes
Direct cognition: No.
Cognitive domain/test: N/A. Authors infer attention-network relevance but did not administer a cognitive task.
Statistics: One-way ANOVA/Tukey HSD for normal data; Kruskal-Wallis/Games-Howell for nonnormal data; Cohen d pairwise effects; Benjamini-Hochberg FDR; p<.05.
p-values/effect sizes: Microstate C differences significant after correction with medium-large Cohen d; exact metric-specific values are available in Results/tables and must be selected before pooling. ML AUC=.956.
Durability: Acute only.

Multiple correction: Yes — Benjamini-Hochberg FDR.
Mood/QOL: None.
Author conclusion: PW 980-nm tPBM specifically reorganizes microstate-C dynamics and networks and may provide a state-dependent biomarker for protocol optimization.
Interpretation: Methodologically strong controlled EEG study with correction and effect sizes, but outcome is microstate/network-specific and cannot be treated as direct cognitive enhancement.
